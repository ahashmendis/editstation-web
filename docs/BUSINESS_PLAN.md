# Business Plan (summary)

Derived from knowledge base §1–3, §7, §12–14 and the owner clarification of 2026-11-11. Source of
truth: `EDITSTATION_MASTER_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md` plus the decisions log in `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## Identity

- Parent business: Lenscape Studios. Operational platform/network: EDITSTATION (working name, not
  trademark-cleared; domain `editstation.lk` availability not verified). See KB §2.2 for checks.
- EDITSTATION is an internal production and operations platform under Lenscape, not a separate
  agency or marketplace.
- It has public entry points _(2026-11-11)_: a website, a client/agency inquiry form, and editor
  registration. A public website makes the brand question (KB §17.2) more pressing.

## Model

1. Clients/agencies reach Lenscape, including through the public inquiry form _(2026-11-11)_.
   Lenscape discusses scope and price with them and keeps the client relationship.
2. Admin converts confirmed work into an internal project: brief, assets, references, deadline,
   proposed editor pay.
3. A vetted editor (registered, applied, admin-approved) is invited/selected, may counteroffer, and
   the final rate is admin-approved.
4. Editor delivers; admin reviews quality, manages revisions, approves.
5. Admin records the editor payment (manual Sri Lankan bank transfer).

## Accounting

Client revenue belongs to Lenscape. Editor compensation is a direct project cost. Never count the
same client revenue under both Lenscape and EDITSTATION. (KB §3.4 example is illustrative only.)

## Pilot

- 5–10 vetted editors; indicative mix 4 short-form, 3 long-form, 2 cinematic, 1 motion graphics.
- Pilot targets (internal, not benchmarks): 10–20 assignments, ≥90% on time, ≥70%
  first-submission approval, <5% editor abandonment, accurate payment records, positive
  contribution per service category. Full metric list: KB §14.
- Scale when quality, reliability and contribution are repeatable, not because more people apply.
  Public registration will attract more applicants than the pilot needs; approval stays selective.

## Legal / admin

KB §12 is a checklist for professional advice (business structure, editor agreement, Sri Lanka PDPA
No. 9 of 2022 as amended, tax). Public forms now collect personal data from non-users (inquiries)
and applicants, so a privacy notice is required before public launch. Nothing in this repo should
state legal or tax conclusions.
