## 2. Your account and access

1. Registering an account gives you no access to work. Access to projects starts only when an admin
   approves and activates you, and ends when you are suspended or revoked.
2. You must give accurate application information and keep it current.
3. Keep your sign-in secure. Do not share your account.
4. [Account suspension and revocation: grounds, notice, appeal: counsel.]

## 3. How work is offered

1. Work is offered to you by an admin, either as a direct invitation or by an admin choosing you
   after you register interest in an opportunity. Registering interest does not guarantee work.
2. You may decline an offer. A proposed rate you enter is only a signal; it never sets your pay.
3. There is one primary editor per project.
4. There is no direct contact with clients through the platform, and you must not approach clients
   for work arranged through EDITSTATION [non-circumvention scope and duration: counsel].

## 4. Pay

1. Your pay for a project is the amount agreed in the app and approved by an admin before you
   start. You must not begin work while your compensation is unresolved.
2. Unless the project's agreed terms say otherwise, pay is in two milestones: 50% when work
   commences (the client deposit has been verified and your assignment confirmed), and 50% after
   final approval and receipt of the client balance.
3. Lenscape pays each milestone by default within [7] calendar days of it becoming payable.
   [Confirm the number and currency.]
4. If the client balance is still unpaid [14] calendar days after it became due, your second
   milestone becomes payable anyway, unless there is a genuine delivery dispute or contractual
   issue.
5. If part of a milestone is disputed, only the disputed portion is held. Undisputed amounts are
   paid.
6. Extra work (an extra paid revision round or a scope change) is offered to you separately, with
   its own agreed amount, and you must accept it in the app before starting it. The original agreed
   amount is never changed.
7. If a client cancels, you are paid for completed work plus any cancellation compensation agreed
   for that project before work began. A client cancellation does not on its own cost you your pay
   for completed work. Any overpayment is corrected by a recorded reversal.
8. Payment is by manual bank transfer. [Currency, taxes, withholding, invoicing and who is
   responsible for tax: counsel; the platform does not give tax advice.]

## 5. Revisions and corrections

1. Fixing a mistake against the approved brief is an editor correction. You are not paid extra for
   it and it does not use a client revision round.
2. A client revision, or a scope change, is classified by an admin. Client revisions may use an
   included round or be chargeable; scope changes are quoted separately.

## 6. Client material and confidentiality

1. Project files are provided by the client as links (for example Google Drive or Dropbox). The
   platform stores links and details, not the media itself.
2. You must keep opportunity summaries, briefs, files and client details confidential, use them
   only for the assigned project, not copy or share them, and delete or stop using them when the
   project ends or you are asked to.
3. Tell an admin if you cannot open a link or receive material you should not have.
4. [Confidentiality duration, return and deletion obligations, remedies: counsel.]
